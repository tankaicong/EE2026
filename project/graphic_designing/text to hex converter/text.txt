Each white pixel(1) is a node
in a grid, neighbours form
edges.
For Left, Upper Left, Upper and
Upper Right pixel, find their
root by walking up their parents.

Allocate current pixel if
neighbours have no roots(it is
a new component).
Else, pick a neighbouring
component and union into
one component.

As frame builds, union
neighbours and grow
components.
Each component stores a running
area count and min, max x, y
for bounding boxes.

Remove tiny components as there
may be noise, by min area.
(no. of pixel in a component)
e.g., 4 means components with
less than 4 pixels are
invalid.

Pick up to 
1 or 2 or 3 or 4
valid components.
Using min, max, centre
of x, y, draw their
respective boxes.
